The brief

Everyone's already made the dystopia movie.

You know the ones. People asleep in goo pods. People on exercise bikes earning points. People too comfortable to get out of their floating chairs. And now the AI version: one company learns every job, does it better, and the stock market is just that one company.

Maybe that's where we're headed. We kind of doubt it. Make the film you think is true. It can be bleak, funny, or weirdly fine. Satire, drama, fake news reports, animation, anything goes.

Rules

Rules

  • New, fresh, hot

    You can bring an idea you've had for years. You can't bring something you already started making.

  • One lead each

    Help out on as many films as you want. You can only lead one.

  • Tell us about the AI

    A few sentences on which tools you used and what for. We want to know.

  • Keep it unlisted

    Don't post it publicly yet. We're putting every film out at the same time after the deadline.

  • English or subtitled

    Any language works if it has English subtitles.

  • The jury decides

    We watch everything, then a jury picks the winner. We'll announce who's on it soon.

Submitting

How to enter

  1. Make it

    5–10 minutes, 4K, between 11 Nov and 14 Dec.

  2. Upload it unlisted

    Put it on YouTube as Unlisted, so only people with the link can see it.

  3. Send us the link

    Plus the lead's name, who else worked on it, and your AI note. We'll post where to send it on 11 Nov.
