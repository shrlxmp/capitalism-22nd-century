The brief

Who owns what when the machines do the work?

We've all seen the usual version. Humans asleep in pods. Humans pedalling exercise bikes for credits. Humans floating around a spaceship, too comfortable to stand up. One AI company trains on every profession, does every job better, and the whole stock market turns into a single ticker.

We're not convinced it goes that way. Show us the economy of the next century as you see it: the dystopia, the boring-but-fine version where companies keep competing, or something nobody has filmed yet. Satire, drama, mockumentary, animation: all welcome.

Rules

The fine print

  • New work only

    Bring an idea you've had for years if you like, but don't submit anything you'd already started making before 11 November.

  • One lead each

    Help out on as many films as you like, but you can be the designated lead on only one submission.

  • Say how you used AI

    Every AI tool is allowed. Include a short note on which ones you used and for what.

  • Keep it unlisted

    Upload to YouTube as an unlisted video and send us the link. Don't post it publicly yet: we'll release every film together after the deadline.

  • English or subtitled

    English dialogue, or English subtitles for any other language.

  • Jury decides

    Selected films screen in London. A jury of filmmakers and writers, to be announced, decides the final award.

How to enter

Three steps. That's it.

  1. Make the film

    5 to 10 minutes, in 4K, made between 11 November and 14 December 2026.

  2. Upload it unlisted

    Post it to YouTube with visibility set to Unlisted, so only people with the link can watch. We'll release them all together.

  3. Send us the link

    With the lead's name, the team, and your AI note. The submission link goes live on 11 November.
